# Video

| Cut | Film | Poster | Render command |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Original, with generated stills | [token-burn.mp4](token-burn.mp4) | [poster.png](poster.png) | `make video` |
| Stock footage | [token-burn-stock.mp4](token-burn-stock.mp4) | [poster-stock.png](poster-stock.png) | `make video/stock` |

Both cuts are 40 seconds, H.264/AAC at 1080 × 1080 and 30 fps, with audio
normalised to -14 LUFS. This directory holds finished viewing copies; source
assets and render instructions live in [`video/`](../../../video/README.md).

The stock cut combines six real lifestyle clips with the original watch
close-ups, narration, captions and end card. Its music continues through the beach
scene and end title, with a final fade. See the [footage credits](../../../video/CREDITS.md).

The watch screens are real Token Burn captures from the Connect IQ simulator, fed with made-up joke numbers rather than anyone's usage.

The root README's embedded GitHub attachment is a lighter re-encode of the
original stills cut (7.8 MB at CRF 22). The MP4 links here are the full-quality exports.
